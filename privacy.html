Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

"Ball League" is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel @BallLeagueDaily to upload videos to that channel.

What data the app uses

The app uses Google OAuth to obtain permission to (1) upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel (youtube.upload) and (2) read basic information about that channel (youtube.readonly), used only to confirm it is uploading to the right channel.

How the data is stored

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google's. The app does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and it does not access other users' data.

Use of Google data

Ball League's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: use the contact details on the YouTube channel @BallLeagueDaily.
